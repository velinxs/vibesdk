# After Hours: research note

Compact record of the terminology pass behind the codex and the encounter rules. The in-game codex (`shared/game/content/codex.ts`) carries the same entries in player-facing form.

## Method

The brief's source register (R1 to R9, checked by the author on 7 September 2026) was taken as the foundation. The build used the definitions the brief already established and verified additions against those sources' published excerpts where they were reachable from the build environment. Source authors' effectiveness claims were treated as claims: the sources document a subculture's vocabulary, not a tested model of attraction. Original routines and dialogue were written for the game; historical commercial scripts were not reproduced.

Accessibility during the build: R1 (publisher excerpt page), R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R8, and R9 were not re-fetched from inside the build sandbox, which has restricted outbound access. Definitions are therefore the brief's, cross-checked against the terms' general usage. Reading an excerpt does not mean a whole book was reviewed.

## Distinctions the engine enforces

| Confusion the comedy exploits | What the engine does |
|---|---|
| Friendly teasing vs. negging | The evaluator labels a put-down as `insult`, a playful jab as `tease`. Only the second is admissible in tease nodes. |
| Calmness vs. "frame control" | Composure scores grounded, non-defensive text. Attempts to reframe a refusal are scored as `pressure`. |
| Disinterest vs. a "test" | Rejection, boundary, and cooling reactions are separate node kinds and reaction tags. No node ever labels disinterest a test. |
| Practical problem vs. rejection | `logistics` nodes exist so a phone at four percent is not a no. |
| Bitch shield | A coach label only, always marked ALLEGED. The node cue is the engine's explanation. |
| Alpha/beta | Coach labels only. Ordinary kindness is never scored as a fault; approval-seeking and defensiveness are scored separately. |

## Terminology adaptations

See the codex for the full table. Notable adaptations:

- Routine names are original: Observation Opener, Agree and Amplify, Playful Reframe, Compact Story, Callback, Direct Interest, Mutual Qualification, Conversational Transition, Playful Shared Scenario, Recovery Acknowledgment, Specific Invitation, Graceful Exit.
- "Kino" is not modeled; typed input cannot touch anyone.
- "False time constraint" is not a routine. Sienna names it when she sees it.
- "Preselection" and "social proof" are difficulty modifiers (starting conditions), never skills.

## What the scoring is and is not

Composure, connection, and calibration are scored from typed words only, with an evidence excerpt and an explanation per turn. Momentum, interest, comfort, and trust are fictional encounter variables. None of this measures real attraction or reads minds, and the debrief says so.
